## What you need
- The PNG you downloaded from the site (it lands in Downloads with a clean name, like `bar-of-gold.png`)
- The folder you want to transform

## The 3 steps

**1. Download the PNG.**
Click Download on any folder you love. That's it — no unzipping, no converting.

**2. Open Get Info on your folder.**
In Finder, select the folder and press ⌘I (or right-click → Get Info).

**3. Drag the PNG onto the little icon.**
In the Get Info window, drag your downloaded PNG directly onto the small folder icon at the very top of the window. Not the big Preview at the bottom — the tiny icon up top. The folder transforms immediately.

Done. Admire your work.

## If dragging feels fiddly — the copy/paste way
1. Double-click the PNG so it opens in Preview.
2. Press ⌘A (selects the whole image), then ⌘C (copies the actual picture).
3. In the Get Info window, click the little icon at the top so it highlights, then press ⌘V.

## To remove it
Get Info → click the little icon → press Delete. Back to classic blue.

## Troubleshooting

**"I pressed ⌘V and nothing happened."**
You copied the file, not the picture. macOS needs the image pixels on the clipboard: open the PNG in Preview first, then ⌘A, ⌘C.

**"I don't see where to drag it."**
It's the tiny icon at the very top of the Get Info window. Not the big Preview image at the bottom — that's just a preview.

**"My folder has an ugly black box around it."**
The PNG needs a transparent background. Re-download it from the site — every image ships background-free. (If you made the image yourself somewhere else, this is the step that bites.)

**"The Dock didn't update."**
Rare, but if a folder in the Dock keeps its old icon: open Terminal, type `killall Dock`, press Return. It relaunches instantly and picks up the new icon.
